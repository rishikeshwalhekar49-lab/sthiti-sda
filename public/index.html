How the score is built

Breadth scores 50 at an advance/decline ratio of 1, 100 at 3 and 0 at ⅓ (log scale). Flows score 50 at zero combined FII + DII flow, ±50 at ±₹6,000 crore. Volatility scores 100 at a VIX of 10 and 0 at 30. The mood is 35% breadth, 30% flows and 35% volatility. These weights are Sthiti's own starting point for testing, not a published standard.

    The market changes state. The lessons should too.

    In plain words

    Every share moves for two reasons: something about that company, and something about the market as a whole. When the second reason takes over, sectors that normally go their own way start moving together — and the ordinary rules stop behaving.

    This page measures that directly, from how closely NSE's sector indices move together. New to the idea? Lesson 11 explains regimes and lesson 12 explains the barcode, both without mathematics.

    Candidates runningAsk the engineCrash replayRegimes → lessonsThe written claims

    Courses teach RSI, stop-losses and valuation as if the market always behaves the same way. It doesn't. The engine takes 18 NSE sector indices, measures how closely they have moved together over a 60-day window, and reads the shape of that correlation structure with persistent homology.

    Each dot is a sector index, placed so that sectors which move together sit close. Persistent homology grows a radius ε around the dots and records when groups merge and when loops open and close. The record is the barcode on the right.

    Drag the window back through the last year and watch the shape change. When correlations rise, groups merge earlier and loops die young.

    • H₀ · groups of sectors that move together. A bar ends when two groups merge.
    • H₁ · loops in the correlation structure. A bar runs from when a loop opens to when it fills in.

    Distances come from the correlation matrix: d = √(2(1 − ρ)) on daily log returns. The dots are a 2-D projection (classical MDS) of those distances, so the picture approximates what the barcode measures exactly.

    Patent candidates

    Seven patent candidates

    Numbered as in the brief: 1 is the strongest candidate under Indian law, 7 the least certain on novelty. Figures are drawn in patent style, with reference numerals.

    1

    Streaming persistent homology engine

    Strongest candidate

    Claim sketchWhen each new bar or tick arrives, update only the simplices it affects instead of rebuilding the diagram, and emit a regime alert within a bounded time and memory budget.

    Recomputing every window from scratch can't keep up with hundreds of assets at intraday frequency.

    Closes
      Novelty watch
      Vineyard-style updating already exists in pure mathematics, so the novelty has to sit in the specific finance pipeline and architecture. Lower latency and memory use is a clear technical effect, which is what Indian law looks for.
      2

      Topological regime attribution

      Nothing comparable found

      Claim sketchWhen a shift is detected, extract the representative cycles and components that caused it, map them back to individual assets and sectors, and output a ranked “who drove this shift” report.

      “The landscape norm spiked” gives a portfolio manager nothing to act on. A ranked list of stocks and sectors does. FIG. 1 runs a toy version: the saffron loop is a representative cycle, read out by sector.

      Closes
        Novelty watch
        Representative cycles are standard TDA output. The claim is the mapping back to assets and the ranking built on it.
        3

        Two-parameter regime fingerprint

        Claim sketchBuild a bifiltration on correlation distance crossed with a liquidity or stress variable, such as bid-ask spread or realized volatility, and define each regime as a region in the resulting multiparameter invariant.

        A liquidity crunch and a correlation crash would finally look different. Single-parameter methods can't tell them apart.

        Closes
          Novelty watch
          Multiparameter persistence has no simple, complete invariant. The claim has to fix a computable one and show that it separates regimes.
          4

          Self-calibrating TDA pipeline

          Claim sketchAutomatically choose window length, embedding dimension, delay and threshold for each market by maximizing diagram stability on calm periods while holding the false-alarm rate below a target, and re-tune when the data's character changes.

          Every early-warning paper admits its results depend on these parameters. Nobody offers automatic, market-specific calibration.

          Closes
            Novelty watch
            Generic hyperparameter search won't carry a claim. The stability objective, the false-alarm constraint and the drift-triggered re-tune, taken together, might.
            5

            Topological drift detector for AI trading models

            Claim sketchTest each topological signal against surrogate null data, such as phase-randomized series, and only when it beats the null, trigger retraining, de-risking or margin changes.

            Practitioners treat detecting shifts in market behaviour as central to model governance, because it signals when a machine learning model needs retraining. This turns a signal into a controlled action.

            Closes
              Novelty watch
              Surrogate testing is established in nonlinear time-series analysis. The claim is the gate between a topological test and a risk action.
              6

              Cross-asset contagion map with zigzag persistence

              Claim sketchTrack cycles as they form and dissolve across a multilayer network linking equities, bonds, FX, commodities and crypto over time, and identify the pathway a shock is taking.

              Zigzag persistence has revealed structure in temporal graphs that standard centrality statistics miss. The published examples are transport networks, not financial contagion.

              Closes
                Novelty watch
                Zigzag persistence itself is known. Applying it to cross-asset contagion paths looks unexplored.
                7

                Topology of the options implied-volatility surface

                Least certain · search hardest

                Claim sketchApply a sublevel-set filtration over the strike × expiry implied-volatility surface and detect regime shifts in derivatives before the cash market reacts.

                NSE's huge options volume makes this a natural fit for Indian data.

                Closes
                  Novelty watch
                  The least certain novelty in the set. Run the hardest search here before spending on drafting.
